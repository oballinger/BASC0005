Explore it: every estimate is a choice of counterfactual

A causal effect is what happened minus what would have happened without the treatment. We never see the second part, so every method fills it in with an assumption, drawn here as the dashed line. Switch between methods and watch the same data give different answers. Then, in difference-in-differences, drag the slider to break the parallel trends assumption and see how much the estimate moves.

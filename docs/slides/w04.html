Exxon's vocabulary as a map

Each dot is one of the 1,500 most Exxon-specific nouns and adjectives in the workshop's 182 transcripts. Each word is a 100-number GloVe embedding (the same vectors as the Week 4 notebook), squashed to 3D with UMAP so nearby dots have similar meanings. Colour = cluster (k-means on the full 100-d vectors), size = how often Exxon says it. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover for the word, or search a word to see its nearest neighbours.

Find a word

Nearest neighbours

Similarity = cosine similarity of the full 100-d vectors (1 = same direction). The 3D map is only an approximation: two dots can look close and not be, or vice versa.
